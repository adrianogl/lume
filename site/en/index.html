Privacy

Everything stays on your Mac.

No account, no analytics, no telemetry. Lume only goes to the network to download your iCal link, if you set one, and to fetch pull requests through the gh you already have logged in.

Download

Three steps and the owl wakes up.

1

Download the zip

Lume-0.1.0.zip, from the GitHub releases page. The SHA-256 comes with it.

2

Move it to Applications

Unzip and drag Lume.app over. It has no Dock icon: it lives in the notch.

3

Right-click to open

The release is not notarized. Right-click › Open. If macOS still refuses: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Lume.app
